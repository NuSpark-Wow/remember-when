📚 Where the photos come from
  • The slide shows look first in the Library of Congress (only photos marked "no known restrictions"), then Wikimedia Commons and Openverse (public domain and free Creative Commons licenses). When none of those truly fits a scene, the best photo from the web is shown, credited with a link to its source; it belongs to its owner. To ask for a photo to be removed, contact NuSpark Publishing at nusparkpublishing.com.
  • Every picture shows its title, photographer or source, and license, with a link to its original page.
  • More free archives worth a look: National Archives, Smithsonian Open Access, New York Public Library Digital Collections, Flickr Commons, and PICRYL.
  • Many famous pictures from these decades, like TV shows, movie stars, album covers, and ads, are still under copyright. The narrator can talk about them, but the slides only show photos that are free to use.

Remember When is hosted by AI, which can make mistakes. Prices and dates are approximate and varied from place to place. Your voice is turned into text by your own browser and is never recorded. Photos you share are used only to make your show and are not saved.
